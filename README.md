## 📂 Product Case Studies

### 🎙️ Fireflies.ai · From Meeting Notes to Closed Commitments: 28% → 45%
**A promise made in a meeting is captured, but owned by no one.**

- **Research:** 31 surveyed, 17 phone interviews, a test meeting with 10 planted commitments, and 8 AI meeting tools compared
- **Insight:** extraction works (7 of 8 commitments caught), but nobody is asked *"is this yours?"*, so nothing has an owner and closure never travels between tools
- **Solution:** *claim once, then Fireflies follows the work*: one-tap *Mine*, tickets arrive already assigned, status syncs two-way across Jira, Asana and Slack
- **AI:** a closure engine matches the next meeting to last week's open commitments (only at ≥ 0.70 confidence, never closing without the owner); 14 sync scenarios tested
- **Rigour:** a model from 28% to 45.6%, RICE across 15 ideas, OKRs and guardrails, a gated 26-week cohort rollout, and $7,800 a year per 1,000 seats

📄 [Read the case study →](case-studies/fireflies-commitment-tracking.md)

### 🚚 Porter · First-Trip Conversion: from 41% to 58%
**Porter asks you to pick the truck before it asks what you're moving.**

- **Research:** 42 user responses (23 followed by calls, across 5 cities), 2 live bookings walked screen by screen, 30 complaints analysed
- **Insight:** labour, not the vehicle, decides the first trip. When labour went as expected, **88%** had a clean first trip; when it broke, only **56%** did
- **Solution:** ask what you're moving first and price the whole trip including labour; replace the cancellation form with a recovery path that resolves the trip
- **Prioritisation and metrics:** RICE across 15 ideas, 1 North Star and 8 guardrails, 3 gated waves
- **Key point:** 4 of the 6 booking changes move features Porter already has rather than build new ones

## 🗂️ More Projects

### 🤖 AI Products & Automation

**⌚ Titan Smart Wellness Coach: turning smartwatch data into one daily action** · *AI product case study (independent)*
- **Problem:** owners see sleep, heart-rate and activity numbers with no interpretation, so the data changes nothing and gives little reason to buy Titan again. 70% of Titan's Q2 2026 smartwatch unit decline is lost share.
- **What I did:** sized the market from IDC data (the ₹4,750–9,500 band grew 117%, ≈₹195 crore a quarter), benchmarked 5 companion apps, scored 4 problems with RICE, built a prototype, and wrote a PRD, a 23-week roadmap for a 9-person team and a ₹1.19 crore business case.
- **Recommendation:** fix sync first (RICE 13.3), then add one daily readiness score with one action (8.3). It pays back at ≈9,800 extra watches; the beta go/no-go is a lift of 10+ points in the weekly active rate.

**⚖️ AI Worth-It Judge: is each AI step worth its cost?** · *Working prototype (Python)*
- **Problem:** teams track AI token cost but not whether reviewers actually use the output.
- **What I did:** simulated a resume-screening workflow (400 resumes × 8 AI steps), called a decision API for keep / tune / swap / remove verdicts with a 0.70 confidence gate that sends unsure cases to human review, built a dashboard, and built a 60-item eval set for a model head-to-head.
- **Finding:** $512/mo of token spend against ≈$7,800/mo of net reviewer time; remove 3 low-value steps (≈$2,400/mo recovered) and test the 3 uncertain ones before switching.

**🎧 AI Support Assistant: deciding what should be a Skill, an Agent, or neither** · *LLM agent design*
- **Problem:** a mid-size SaaS company runs support by hand and wants AI on 5 workflow pieces: triage, replies, retention briefings, QA grading and bug write-ups.
- **What I did:** built a 3-question framework to classify each piece; built 3 Claude skills (ticket triage, reply voice, bug write-up); designed 2 agents and tested the QA agent on 7 tickets; built the retention agent as an n8n workflow calling the Anthropic API.
- **Key call:** outage, legal and security escalation should **not** use an LLM. A deterministic keyword and status-page check is faster and safer where a miss costs the most.

**📰 Competitor News Digest: a scored digest every morning at 8 AM** · *n8n automation*
- Built a 15-node n8n workflow: competitors from Google Sheets → 5 articles each from NewsAPI → dedupe → Gemini 2.0 Flash summarises, categorises and scores each one (articles under 6 dropped) → delivered to Slack and Gmail and logged to a history sheet. The whole stack runs on free tiers.

**🛡️ PulseReel: a safety layer for viral civic content** · *Pitch + PRD, trust & safety*
- **Problem:** a short-video feed treats fast-spreading civic content like entertainment, which risks unverified mobilisation and localised platform blocks in India.
- **What I did:** wrote 3 personas (creator, safety lead, legal director) and a non-blocking flow: a flag when shares grow 300% in 5 min or views pass 10K in 30 min with sensitive keywords, then a 15-minute AI check behind a soft banner, then an email appeal. Wrote 3 engineering tickets.
- **Targets:** 65% drop in viral reach of unverified videos within 72 hours, under 20 min median appeal turnaround, no churn among flagged creators. Posting stays instant, so it never feels like a shadowban.

### 📈 Product Strategy & Growth

**🏢 B2B AI Customer-Operations Platform: closing a 14-hour support-to-engineering gap** · *Product strategy*
- Support runs 24/7 but engineering works business hours, so tickets wait ≈14 hours and arrive without context. I compared 3 segments and chose companies with 200–1,000 employees, then defined a land-and-expand plan: a free tier that integrates in under an hour, then 4 add-ons (log fetcher, multi-app actions, SLA monitor, churn detector), pricing tied to tickets processed, and a 3-year roadmap.
- **Key insight:** the biggest delay is the handoff, not the fix. AI should prepare engineering-ready tickets, not replace support teams.

**🏠 Airbnb India: product-led growth strategy** · *Growth / PLG*
- India is an acquisition story: nights booked from India grew ≈50% YoY and first-time bookers 75%+. I set out motions for 4 functions and 5 product loops, designed 3 MVPs with hypotheses, cheap prototypes and success metrics, and proposed 6 revenue lines.
- **Key insight:** remove friction for first-time group travellers and new tier-2/3 hosts (regional language, fees, WhatsApp-based planning, state-by-state compliance), then let each booking bring in the next user. North Star: successful India-origin trips.

**📊 Zomato Tier-1 Engagement & Retention Study** · *Metrics / retention*
- In Tier-1 cities awareness is saturated, so the fight is for the next order. I ran a 7-stage funnel analysis with a drop-off hypothesis per stage, 3 cohort comparisons and a metric ladder for 10 hypotheses, with **Orders per Active User per Month** as the North Star.
- **Key insight:** discovery (decision fatigue) and checkout (fee shock) are the biggest leaks; the order-1-to-order-2 gap is the biggest retention lever; and discounts build loyalty to the deal, not to Zomato.

**🛒 Zepto: raising average order value without slowing shopping** · *Product case study, team of 2*
- With a teammate, ran user interviews plus social-media research, then built a persona, journey map, 5 Whys analysis and risk table.
- **Key insight:** the free-delivery threshold isn't the root cause; the gap is contextual discovery. Recommended 2–3 add-on suggestions in context (bread → butter, jam). It scored 144 on RICE vs 96 for progressive rewards.

**🎓 Personalised Learning Recommendation System**
- Wrote 15 user stories across 3 personas mapped to 10 prioritised features, plus non-functional requirements (180 ms p95, 50K concurrent users, GDPR, WCAG 2.1 AA), a 3-layer architecture, a risk register and a 9-FTE, $1.4M, 6-month plan.
